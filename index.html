User Guide & Instructions

Planar grid calibration & real-world measurement

What It Is & What It's Used For

GridMeasure rectifies optical and perspective distortions (camera tilt, rotation, skew, scale) using a 4-point 2D projective transformation (homography). Runs 100% client-side with zero external dependencies.

🔬
Specimen & Lab Leaves, insects, seed sizes, or petri dish samples on millimeter grid paper.
📐
Engineering & Inspection PCB traces, component tolerances, and planar materials sizing.
🔍
Forensic & Field Work Objects photographed in situ with a planar scale marker or grid.
📊
Auditable Data Export Coordinates formatted for exact mathematical pixel reconstruction.

Step-by-Step Workflow

1

Load Images

Click Add Images in the left sidebar (supports PNG, JPEG, WebP, TIFF). Thumbnails show calibration status:

No border: 0 points Yellow border: 1–4 points (calibrating) Green border: 5+ points (ready)
2

Calibrate Grid (Points P1–P4)

Click the 4 corners of a single known grid square in counter-clockwise order:

P4 (0, 1)
P3 (1, 1)
Known Grid Square
P1 (0, 0)
P2 (1, 0)
  • P1 → (0,0): Reference origin
  • P2 → (1,0): One unit along the horizontal X-axis
  • P3 → (1,1): Opposite diagonal corner
  • P4 → (0,1): One unit along the vertical Y-axis
Placing P4 automatically computes the homography matrix and displays real-time calibration diagnostics.
3

Set Physical Units

Under Calibration in the right sidebar, enter your grid unit size (e.g. 10 for 10 mm grid spacing). All measurements will display converted physical units in millimeters (mm).

4

Place Measurement Points (P5+)

Click features of interest on the calibrated image. Consecutive points (P5–P6, P7–P8, etc.) automatically compute and display straight-line Euclidean distance in both grid units and millimeters.

5

Interactive Adjustments & Controls

  • Magnifier Loupe: Hover over the canvas to activate the zoom loupe with crosshairs for sub-pixel precision.
  • Drag to Reposition: Click and drag any point to fine-tune its position. Moving P1–P4 instantly recalculates the entire calibration and all downstream measurements.
  • Delete / Reset: Click the × button on any point, use Delete Last to pop the newest point, or click Reset Points to clear the image.
6

Export & Audit Data

  • Export CSV: Downloads a new CSV containing image metadata, pixel coordinates, calibrated positions, and calculated distances.
  • Append to CSV: Select an existing CSV file to append records from additional images, creating a consolidated dataset.

Calibration Diagnostics Explained

Once P1–P4 are placed, 5 diagnostic cards appear in the right sidebar to assess calibration quality:

Metric What It Measures Interpretation
Rotation Angle of vector P1 → P2 relative to image horizontal Grid tilt relative to camera sensor (e.g. +2.4°). Compensated automatically.
Scale Pixel resolution per unit (px/unit) & physical density (px/mm) Higher values = greater precision. Compares X vs Y resolution (U × V).
Aspect Ratio Ratio of vertical to horizontal unit length (|P4-P1| / |P2-P1|) 1.000 is ideal (Square ✓). Deviations indicate non-square pixels or angled tilt.
Corner Angle Angle between P1 → P2 and P1 → P4 90.0° is ideal (Orthogonal ✓). Deviations highlight shear skew or perspective slant.
Perspective Homography projective magnitude & keystone ratio Categorized None to Strong. Confirms perspective foreshortening correction.

Export Format & Complete Reconstruction

GridMeasure exports image metadata, raw pixel coordinates for calibration points P1–P4, and calibrated grid coordinates for measurement points P5+.

📐
Because the 3×3 homography matrix H is invertible, downstream scripts can analytically calculate H-1 to reconstruct exact original pixel coordinates on the raw image, guaranteeing full scientific auditability.
